Welcome to Re:Color!

A paint app for people with color-vision difficulties.

Enter the digital canvas

What it does

re-color helps users manage, preview, and switch color palettes with ease. It is built to support designers, developers, and creative teams who want faster color iteration and cleaner visual consistency across interfaces.

Key features

  • Palette creation and management
  • Live previews for color combinations
  • Accessible contrast checking
  • Export-ready color values for CSS and design tools

Why re-color exists

Choosing the right colors is a critical part of building modern interfaces. re-color was created to reduce the friction around testing palettes and to make color decisions more intentional, reliable, and repeatable.

Getting started

To begin using re-color, explore the project interface and load or create a palette. Experiment with different combinations until you find the visual system that fits your product or brand.
